## Review Focus

1. **Domain A (Regex Safety & Parity)**: JS `engine.js` and `utils.js` out-of-range `$1`/`$2` group tokens on 0-group/1-group regexes must resolve to `""` (not match `offset` or `inputString`), and static + runtime ReDoS guards must catch `/(a+){30}b/g`, `/(a+){5}b/g`, `/(a+)+b/g`, `/(\w+)+b/g`, `/((a+))+b/g`, `/(a|aa)+b/g`, and `/(a+|b)+c/g` under killable child-process timeouts while keeping Kotlin `BoundedRegexCharSequence` (`250,000` steps / `50ms`).
2. **Domain B (WebView Pool & Lifecycle)**: `useBoundedWebViewPool` must remain enabled by default (`DEFAULT_SAFE_MAX_ACTIVE_WEBVIEWS = 6` when `!perfEnabled`), `isHibernated` and `AndroidView.onRelease` must evict and release panels once evicted from `activePoolKeys` across all modes, switching conversations (`TavernRuntimeManager.setActiveConversation`) must release previous conversation panels, cancelled scheduler requests must cancel their gate, delayed ready probes must be cancelled on ready/dispose/reload and never reload an already-mounted panel, and streaming DOM coalescing must not drop the final update.
3. **Domain C (Generation Epoch & Cancel+Resend Race)**: `ChatService.stopGeneration` must not clobber or cancel a newly started resend job after awaiting `job.join()`, while preserving `generationEpoch` fencing in `ConversationSession`/`ChatService`, `CancellationException` rethrowing in `quietGenerate()`, canceled `IOException` conversion in `GenerationLoop.awaitNetworkRetryOrThrow`, and per-stage `isEpochAborted()` checks in `runtime.js`.
4. **Domain D (Slider Resolution & UI)**: Verify `ReasoningSliderSpec` in `ReasoningPicker.kt`, `LevelPicker.kt`, and `ReasoningSliderSpecTest.kt` resolves cleanly and preserves Kelivo geometry, drag, spring snap, and haptics.
5. **Domain E (Updater, Migrations & Reproducible Fixtures)**: Unify `UpdateChecker.kt`/`UpdateCard.kt` validations (`UpdateCheckerValidationTest.kt` + `UpdateCheckerTest.kt`), port `PreferenceStoreV5Migration.kt` inferred-OpenAI `balanceOption` migration & string/object log redaction, verify empty/populated/idempotent/interrupted SQLite Room migrations from production Kotlin SQL, and make optional external `ALL_NEEDS_DIR` plugin tests output explicit `# SKIP` (with reason and command) when absent while failing if an explicitly provided `ALL_NEEDS_DIR` is missing expected files.
